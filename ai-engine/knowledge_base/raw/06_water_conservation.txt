Water Conservation in Irrigation

Efficient water use protects both crop health and limited water resources. Common water conservation practices include:

- Drip irrigation, which delivers water directly to the root zone with minimal loss to evaporation or runoff, is generally more water-efficient than flood irrigation.
- Mulching (covering soil with organic material like straw) reduces evaporation from the soil surface and helps retain moisture longer.
- Irrigating during early morning or evening reduces water loss to evaporation compared to midday watering.
- Avoiding overwatering not only saves water but also protects the crop from waterlogging and nutrient leaching.
- Grouping crops with similar water needs together makes irrigation scheduling more efficient than watering a mixed field uniformly.

Monitoring actual soil moisture, rather than watering on a fixed routine, is one of the most effective ways to conserve water while keeping the crop healthy.
