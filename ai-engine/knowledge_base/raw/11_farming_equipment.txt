Common Farming Equipment by Purpose

Land preparation equipment:
- Tractor: primary power source for most mechanized field operations.
- Plough: breaks and turns soil before sowing.
- Cultivator: loosens soil and helps control weeds before sowing.
- Harrow: breaks up soil clumps after ploughing for a finer seedbed.

Irrigation equipment:
- Water pump: draws water from a well, borewell, or canal to the field.
- Pipes: carry water from the pump to the field or irrigation system.
- Drip irrigation system: includes drippers, main lines, and filters - delivers water directly to the root zone, water-efficient.
- Sprinkler system: includes sprinkler heads and pipes - sprays water over a wider area, suited to certain crops and soil types.
- Filters and valves: prevent clogging in drip/sprinkler systems and help control water flow.

Crop management equipment:
- Sprayer: applies pesticides or fertilizers evenly across the field.
- Seeder/planter: sows seeds at a consistent depth and spacing.
- Weeder: removes weeds between crop rows, manually or mechanically.

Harvesting equipment:
- Reaper: cuts mature crops like wheat or rice.
- Thresher: separates grain from harvested stalks.
- Combine harvester: performs cutting and threshing together, mainly used for large-scale cereal farming.

The right equipment depends on farm size, crop type, and budget - a small farm may rely more on manual or shared/rented equipment, while larger operations benefit more from owned mechanized equipment.
