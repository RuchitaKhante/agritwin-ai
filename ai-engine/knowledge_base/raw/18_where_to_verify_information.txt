Where Farmers Can Verify Current Prices, Brands, and Product Information

This chatbot does not have access to live, current prices or a verified list of commercial brands, since these change frequently and vary by region. For accurate, up-to-date information, farmers should check the following types of official sources:

Fertilizer and pesticide prices and availability:
- The nearest Krishi Seva Kendra (agricultural input dealer) can confirm current local prices and available brands.
- The Department of Fertilizers, Government of India, publishes subsidized fertilizer prices (for urea, DAP, MOP, and NPK complexes) on its official website.
- Local agricultural cooperative societies (e.g. PACS) often have current stock and pricing information.

Crop/mandi (market) prices:
- The Agmarknet portal (agmarknet.gov.in), run by the Government of India, publishes daily mandi prices for various crops across different markets.
- The eNAM (National Agriculture Market) portal (enam.gov.in) provides price information from electronic trading platforms.
- The local mandi committee (Krishi Upaj Mandi Samiti) can give the most immediate local price.

Pesticide/fungicide/herbicide brand and registration information:
- The Central Insecticides Board & Registration Committee (CIBRC) maintains the official list of registered pesticides in India, which confirms a product is legally approved for use.
- A licensed pesticide dealer can confirm available brands and current stock in your area.

Fertilizer recommendations and soil testing:
- The nearest Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) or state agricultural university extension center can provide soil testing services and location-specific fertilizer recommendations.
- The Soil Health Card scheme (soilhealth.dac.gov.in) provides free soil testing and fertilizer recommendations to registered farmers in India.

General agricultural advisory:
- The Kisan Call Centre (toll-free: 1800-180-1551) provides free agricultural advice from experts in regional languages.

These sources are current as of the chatbot's knowledge and should be verified, since government portal names and contact details can change over time.
